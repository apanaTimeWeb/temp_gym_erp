# Admin Reports — Forbidden Patterns

- Do not own an Attendance, Sales, Finance, Billing, HR, or Members workflow under Reports merely because its screen is a report.
- Do not duplicate business APIs, stores, schemas, fixtures, or permissions from owning domains.
- Do not synthesize routes or external API contracts.
- Do not persist business entities in the Reports catalog.
- FORBIDDEN: frontend compatibility redirects and alias maps; use canonical routes only.
